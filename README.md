Head Over Heels

A two-player fighting game about winning the girl, and maybe losing your heart in the process.

Game Jam Theme: Head Over Heels

About the Game

Head Over Heels is a local 1v1 fighting game. Two players battle it out round after round, and the first player to win 5 rounds is the victor.

The winner earns the grand prize: they get to free the girl from the cage.

But the fight isn't the whole story. Along the way, the two rivals begin to fall for each other, so what starts as a brawl turns into something a lot more romantic.

The Story
Two fighters face off, and a girl is locked in a cage overhead.
Whoever wins 5 rounds gets to open the cage and take her out.
As the rounds go on, the two players end up falling head over heels for each other.
Gameplay
Mode: 2-player local versus
Win condition: First player to win 5 rounds
Reward: The winner takes the girl out of the cage
